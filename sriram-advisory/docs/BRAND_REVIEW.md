# Sriram Advisory brand and commercial review

Reviewed 11 October 2026 against the local implementation.

## Verdict

The homepage and core advisory journey present a professional, founder-led consulting practice. The full website still mixes that identity with an older career intelligence and digital-product brand. The highest-value next work is clearer buyer focus, substantiated experience, visible sample outputs and consistency across retained pages.

This is an editorial assessment, not evidence of conversion performance. Reviewed rendered text and responses across 70 public routes (62 sitemap entries plus eight additional entry points), source content and desktop screenshots of Home, About, Work with Sriram, Resources and the Career Risk Audit. All visited routes returned 200 after redirects. This does not verify every interactive state, paid fulfilment, private report contents, account settings or external research claims. No public copy or commercial terms were changed during this review.

## What already works

- Founder name, portrait, first-person copy and “Work with Sriram” make the person behind the practice visible.
- Restrained typography, colours and spacing suit an experienced adviser. Core pages are readable and avoid inflated agency language.
- The worked training/interruptions example demonstrates reasoning and connects it to observable progress. Its illustrative label is honest.
- Sprint deliverables are concrete: problem brief, decision note and 30-day action plan.
- Diagnosis, implementation and workshops form a coherent engagement progression. Responsibility and scope boundaries support a sustainable solo practice.
- The three new essays demonstrate a consistent point of view on decisions, work and learning.
- Enquiries do not require a perfectly defined problem. The email alternative and privacy acknowledgement support trust.

## Proposed positioning direction

“Independent consulting for IT services leaders facing recurring delivery problems, unclear technology decisions and changes that are difficult to put into practice.”

Supporting founder statement, subject to owner approval: “You work directly with me from the first discussion through the agreed review. I bring 30 years across delivery, operations, quality and capability building.”

Use the client relationship and personal accountability as the brand promise. “Solo consultant” is useful for clarity; “solopreneur” need not be the customer-facing headline. The buyer's problem and the value of Sriram's judgment should lead.

## Recommended sequence

1. Reconcile conflicting commercial promises and review evidence-sensitive claims.
2. Sharpen homepage audience/context and explicitly explain direct work with Sriram.
3. Add verified experience and permissioned proof to About and the advisory journey.
4. Add sample outputs and clarify Sprint versus Health Check; confirm practical engagement expectations.
5. Harmonise retained voice and templates, improve framework destinations and add consulting resources.

Confirm effectiveness with buyer interviews and enquiry quality after release. No increase in conversion or revenue is assumed from this editorial review.
