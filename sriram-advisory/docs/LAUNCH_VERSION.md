# Consulting launch version

The homepage connects the owner's published thinking, recognisable business problems, an illustrative worked example and a clear Sprint entry point. The repetitive perspective section has been removed.

Three fictional scenarios are published at /thinking-in-practice. These demonstrate reasoning rather than assert customer results, endorsements or consulting history. The full texts live in content/problem-solving-examples.ts.

The Sprint page shows an illustrative engagement format, including a two-to-three-week planning example and two-to-four hours of sponsor involvement. These are planning assumptions, not historical delivery statistics or firm commitments. Actual schedule, participation and fees require the agreed proposal. No response SLA, price, testimonial or result is invented.

The existing Thinking collection provides the body of writing; no new ghostwritten pieces are misrepresented as prior LinkedIn posts. The real supplied LinkedIn attribution remains linked.

Before public launch, the owner should review whether the proposed planning format matches available capacity. Replace illustrative scenarios with clearly sourced, approved experience stories as those become available.

Buyer validation remains real-world work: ask five prospective buyers what they would call Sriram for, what they expect to receive, and what prevents an enquiry. No research results are claimed in this version.
